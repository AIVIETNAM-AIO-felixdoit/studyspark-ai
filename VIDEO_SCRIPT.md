# Demo video script (English, about 2 minutes)

Record: Windows Game Bar (Win + Alt + R) or Clipchamp. Upload to YouTube as Unlisted.

1. (0:00) Show the home page with the buildathon sticker in the navbar.
   "Hi, I'm [name]. This is StudySpark AI. Students in Bangladesh spend hours turning long notes into something they can actually study from. StudySpark does it in about ten seconds."
2. (0:15) Click "Try sample notes", choose level HSC, click Generate.
   "I paste my notes, choose my level and language, English or Bangla, and click Generate."
3. (0:30) Summary tab.
   "The AI reads only my notes and returns a summary, key terms and exam tips."
4. (0:45) Flashcards tab, flip 2 to 3 cards, press Shuffle.
   "It also makes flashcards for active recall. Tap to flip."
5. (1:05) Quiz tab, answer 2 to 3 questions (get one wrong on purpose).
   "Then it quizzes me with instant feedback and explanations."
6. (1:25) Ask AI tab: type "Explain the Calvin cycle like I'm 12".
   "And I can ask follow-up questions. The tutor answers from my own notes."
7. (1:45) Switch output to Bangla and generate again (optional).
   "It's free, works on any phone, and needs no sign-up. Thank you!"
