Problem Statement

Students drown in notes,
but can't turn them into learning.

  • Millions of SSC, HSC and university students in Bangladesh study from long lecture notes, PDFs and guidebooks.
  • Making summaries, flashcards and practice questions by hand takes hours, so most students just re-read (the least effective way to study).
  • Private tutors and coaching centres are expensive and not available 24/7.
1 / 5
Proposed Solution

⚡ StudySpark AI

A free web app that turns any notes into a complete study kit in about 10 seconds, in English or Bangla.

📝 SummaryKey ideas, key terms and exam tips
🃏 FlashcardsTap-to-flip cards for active recall
✅ QuizMCQs with instant scoring and explanations
💬 Ask AIA tutor that answers using your notes

No sign-up, no install, works on any phone browser.

How the Solution Works

From notes to study kit

1. InputPaste notes or upload a .txt; pick language and level
→
2. AI generationLLM (GPT-4o-mini via Puter.js) with a structured prompt grounded only in the notes
→
3. Structured JSONSummary, terms, tips, 8 flashcards, 5 MCQs, validated and retried if invalid
→
4. Interactive UIFlip cards, take the quiz, chat with the AI tutor
  • Tech: HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Puter.js AI API (no server, no API key cost), hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Adapts to the student: high school / HSC / university level, English or Bangla output.
